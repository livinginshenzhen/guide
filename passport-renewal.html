Passport & Consulate Guide

There is no US Consulate in Shenzhen. The nearest is in Guangzhou, reachable by high-speed train. To renew the passport of any minor or any person with a 5-year validity passport, you'll need to go to the Consulate in person. For minors, both parents should plan to attend or bring sufficient consent documents if only one parent can come.

Consulate Prohibits Electronic Devices

No phones, smartwatches, or any electronics are allowed inside the consulate building. Staying within walking distance lets you leave devices in your hotel room. The consulate does offer free phone storage inside (they hand you a cubby token) – but smartwatches must stay behind entirely.

What To Expect at Renewal Appointment

1

Arrive based on your appointment time, not early.

Lines form early but entry is by slot. Arriving at 7:00 for an 8:45 appointment just means more standing around.

2

Ignore phone-storage vendors outside – the consulate stores them free.

They hand you a cubby token inside the building.

3

Use your US passport to skip the non-citizen queue.

After the outdoor checkpoint, head left to the Americans-only lane – bypasses the student-visa line entirely.

4

Passport services on Floor 2. Credit cards accepted (Visa / Mastercard).

Expect 4 separate desk visits:

Desk 1

Review DS-11 form + shipment label. Passport photos stapled here.

Desk 2

Fill in Chinese address (from police registration doc) + write tracking number on the green slip. Submit all photocopies.

Desk 3

Payment (US credit cards are accepted)

Desk 4

Brief interview with a US consulate staff member.

What To Do With Your Expiring Passport

Option A – Keep It & Mail Later

Take your expired passport home with you after the appointment. When your new passport is ready (4–8 weeks), mail the expired one to the consulate via EMS.

Best if you have travel planned – you can keep using your old passport in the meantime.

Option B – Leave It There

Leave the expired passport at the consulate with your application. Simpler, but you won't have it for travel until the new one arrives.

Best if you have no travel planned before the new passport arrives.

Note: High-speed train travel in China requires your passport for booking and boarding. If you leave your expired passport at the consulate, you won't have any passport on hand – which may make this option unworkable if any train travel is anticipated before the new one arrives.

Either way: Both passports return to you via EMS, requiring payment on delivery – around ¥22. You'll need to be home, or your building's front desk can receive and pay on your behalf.

After You Receive Your New Passport

Two more required steps once it arrives:

1. Update TRRF at Your Police Station

All foreigners must update their Temporary Residence Registration Form at their local police station after any passport change.

2. Renew Your Visa at the Civic Center

Visa expiration dates are tied to the passport they were issued in. You must renew your visa after getting a new passport. It's recommended to book an appointment.
