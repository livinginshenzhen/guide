Passport & Consulate Guide

There is not a US Consulate in Shenzhen. The nearest is in Guangzhou, reachable by high-speed train from Futian. To renew any minor's passport (or anyone with a passport with an initial 5-year validity), you need to visit the Consulate in person. For minors, both parents should plan to attend or bring sufficient consent documents if only one parent can come.

Before Renewal Appointment

Consulate Prohibits Electronic Devices

No phones, smartwatches, or any electronics are allowed inside the consulate building. Staying within walking distance lets you leave devices in your hotel room. The consulate does offer free phone storage inside (they hand you a cubby token) – but smartwatches must stay behind entirely.

Answering the Passport Validity Question

When using the Consular Services Tool, you'll be asked if the applicant has the passport in their possession. Is the validity ten (10) years, five (5) years, or one (1) year or less.

This question is about the validity at issuance – not the remaining validity.

What To Expect at Renewal Appointment

1

Arrive a few minutes before your appointment time.

You may see lines, but they are usually for the visa applicants – not citizen services appointments. There are three gates. One is for officials, one is for exiting, and the northeast gate is for entry. Use this Amap link to navigate to No.43, Huaxia Road.

2

Helpful to Know: There will be storage, so you can ignore phone storage aunties outside.

Inside, they'll store a few of your items (e.g., phones, umbrella). Just remember, you can't and won't have a phone inside during your appointment to confirm your address or other information.

3

Here's what to expect at the three entry checkpoints.

  1. First, right off the sidewalk, show your printed appointment confirmation.
  2. Second, at the outdoor checkpoint, show your passport where others are showing their Chinese ID. Then you'll head toward the American Consular Services checkpoint.
  3. Third, at the checkpoint inside, you'll show your passports and go through airport-like security and hand over any bags or electronic devices to be stored.
  4. Then you'll head inside to Floor 2 to find passport services.
4

Here's how to be prepared for the four counters.

Desk 1

You don't need a number to step to the first desk. You'll get a number there (e.g, M100) that will be used to call you to the other desks. The official will collect the DS-11 form. Your passport photos stapled here, and all the documents collected.

Desk 2

This official will review all your documents and ask for any necessary duplicates. You'll complete a mailing label to ship your new passport. (This is where you'll need your printed address in characters). Tip: Write that tracking number on the green slip.

Desk 3

You'll pay your fee. US credit cards are accepted including Visa and Mastercard.

Desk 4

This agent will conduct a brief interview.

What to Expect After Your Renewal Appointment

After successfully submitting the application for the new passport, processing may take 6–8 weeks. (Under emergency situations, it could take as little as 4 weeks.) Decide which of two options is easier for providing the expiring passport for cancelation – take it with you or leave it with the Consulate.

Option A – Keep It & Mail Later

Take your expired passport home with you after the appointment. When your new passport is ready (4–8 weeks), mail the expired one to the consulate via EMS.

Best if you have travel planned – you can keep using your old passport in the meantime.

Option B – Leave It There

Leave the expired passport at the consulate with your application. Simpler, but you won't have it for travel until the new one arrives.

Best if you have no travel planned before the new passport arrives.

Note: High-speed train travel in China requires your passport for booking and boarding. If you leave your expired passport at the consulate, you won't have any passport on hand – which may make this option unworkable if any train travel is anticipated before the new one arrives.

Either way: Both passports return to you via EMS, requiring payment on delivery – around ¥22. You'll need to be home, or your building's front desk can receive and pay on your behalf.

After You Receive Your New Passport

Two more required steps once it arrives:

1. Update TRRF at Your Police Station

All foreigners must update their Temporary Residence Registration Form at their local police station after any passport change.

2. Renew Your Visa at the Civic Center

Visa expiration dates are tied to the passport they were issued in. You must renew your visa after getting a new passport. It's recommended to book an appointment.
