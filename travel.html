Living in Shekou makes domestic and international travel much easier.

On the day of your arrival and before you enter, find the WeChat mini program and submit this (for every member in your party). It replaces the paper arrival card and speeds up immigration.

Know your vessel or flight number.

Hong Kong Airport → Shenzhen Shekou

Step-by-step ferry guide for transit passengers arriving at HKG and continuing to Shenzhen via the SkyPier direct ferry – no Hong Kong immigration required.

Through-Checked Baggage Credit Cards Accepted
No Hong Kong Immigration Required

The HKG SkyPier is located airside – inside the restricted transit zone. Transit passengers bypass Hong Kong immigration entirely, saving significant time. This is the primary reason to use this route.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Before Departure: Fill Out China's Electronic Entry Card

Before you leave, complete China's electronic entry card online and save the QR code it generates. You'll use it during immigration on arrival in Shekou – even though your passport will usually pull up your info automatically, it's good to have as a backup.

2

Deplane → Follow Signs to "Immigration"

Upon exiting the aircraft, follow overhead signs towards Immigration / 入境檢查. Look for signs also showing Mainland/Macao (內地/澳門) – this is the direction you want.

You will see blue directional signs overhead. The key indicators are: Immigration, Transfer Desks E1, E2, and Mainland/Macao.

3

Board the Internal Airport Train

You must take a short automated train to reach the immigration and ferry terminal area. Follow the train icons on the signage.

Once off the train, continue following signs for Mainland/Macao (內地/澳門). You'll look for Transfer Desk E2 on the left.

4

Locate the Ferry Ticket Counter

Look for the CKS SkyPier Ferry Ticket Counter. Do not clear Hong Kong immigration. The counter handles ferries to both Macau and Shenzhen (Shekou).

Go to the counter designated for Shekou / 蛇口 (Shenzhen). Do not join the Macau queue by mistake.

5

Purchase Your Ticket to "Shekou" & Inform the Agent

Ask for the next available ferry to Shekou (蛇口). At the counter, you will hand over your checked baggage tags (if you did not have the bags originally checked through to ZYK). The ferry service will collect and load your bags directly onto the ferry. You will NOT collect your bags in Hong Kong. You pick up your checked luggage only after arriving at Shekou.

The SkyPier ferry accepts credit cards – you do not need to exchange cash before boarding.

6

Take Train to Airside Ferry Terminal

At the appointed time, you'll show your ticket and then take the internal airport train to the airside ferry terminal. Note that there is a 7-Eleven outlet near the ferry gates.

7

Board the Ferry & Cross to Shekou

The ferry journey takes approximately 45–60 minutes. The ferry is comfortable with indoor seating. Your carry-on luggage can be stowed near the entry doors.

8

Collect Your Baggage

Upon arrival at Shekou Ferry Terminal (蛇口客运港), proceed through immigration. Then, downstairs via an escalator, collect your checked bags from the baggage belt at the terminal.

9

Head to the Ride Hailing Area

After Customs, take the elevator to the 西 West Ride Hailing Area and order a Didi.

Important Reminders

Arrive at the Ferry Counter at Least 45 Minutes Before Departure

The baggage handover, ticket processing, and boarding procedure take time. Missing the ferry cutoff is a real risk if you linger. Move briskly from your gate to the SkyPier area.

Through-Checked Baggage Is a Major Convenience

The SkyPier service allows your checked luggage to be transferred directly from your incoming flight to the ferry – you never handle your bags at HKG. Just hand over your baggage tags at the ticket counter and collect at Shekou. Confirm this service is active when you purchase your ticket.

Backup Plan – If You Miss the Ferry or Accidentally Clear Immigration in Hong Kong
Critical: Once You Clear Hong Kong Immigration, You Cannot Return to the SkyPier Ferry

The ferry terminal is airside. Clearing HK immigration means you have exited the restricted zone – there is no re-entry to take the ferry. You must use an alternative route.

Alternative Route via Shenzhen Bay Checkpoint

CTG Bus (中旅巴士) – Mainland/Macao Transport Area

1
Clear Hong Kong immigration (if not already done) and collect your checked baggage from the baggage reclaim carousel.
2
Clear Hong Kong Customs and proceed to the Arrivals Hall.
3
Follow signs to "Mainland/Macao Transport" (往內地/澳門交通) – located in the ground transportation area, signposted from the Arrivals Hall.
4
Find the CTG Bus (中旅巴士) ticket counter. Purchase a ticket specifying your destination: Shenzhen, Shekou, crossing via the Shenzhen Bay (深圳灣) checkpoint. The agent will direct you to the correct bus bay (Bay 32–34 area for limos/coaches).
5
The bus/limo will cross the Shenzhen Bay Bridge border crossing. Expect the total journey to take approximately 1.5–2.5 hours depending on border wait times – significantly longer than the ferry.

Traveling from Shekou

Ferry from Shekou Port

Buy tickets via the GBA Ferries 粤港快船 (Yuè Gǎng Kuài Chuán) WeChat mini program. The ferry goes to Hong Kong (Central), Macao, Dong'an Island, and other destinations on the bay.

Headed to Hong Kong Int'l Airport?

Check your bags at the Shekou Ferry Terminal. They travel with the ferry and meet you at your final flight destination – no re-claiming bags at HK airport!

Refunds on Tickets from Shekou to HKG

A refund (paid in HKD) is paid to foreigners that travel to HKG from Shekou Ferry Terminal. A desk just after going through HKG airport security will issue HKD$200 for each ticket – and that cash is readily usable in Hong Kong and Macau.

Shenzhen Bao'an Airport (SZX)

Your primary airport for domestic China travel and international flights. Easy access from Shekou.

e-Channel Fast-Track Hong Kong Immigration

Frequent HK airport travelers should apply for the e-Channel pass – skip the immigration line and save your passport pages from extra stamps.

Applicants must be 12 years or older.
