Working

Be aware of the days the office will be closed and the hours teams typically keep.

The Work Day

Although tech companies in Shenzhen, specifically, seem to take pride in the “996” philosophy – working 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., 6 days a week – it doesn’t entirely apply to every company.

Shenzhen (Typical Working Hours)

Company’s U.S. Office Hours (as Observed)

You’ll find most employees arrive at 9 a.m. and leave at 6 or 7 p.m. Lunch usually is taken at 11:30 a.m., followed by a nap or walk until 1:30 p.m. Note that scheduling meetings during this period should be avoided. Generally, the higher the level, the less this pattern is followed.

Work Calendar

Although you’ll have unlimited paid time off, be aware of the days the office will be closed and teams will not be working. You may also consider not traveling domestically during these periods – or risk traveling with extreme crowds.

The company will release its official calendar only after the government releases mandatory minimum days off directive at the end of each year. Practically, that means the exact holiday dates for Chinese New Year aren’t known until the end of the previous year – only a month or two prior.

Determining Office Holiday Calendar

Some Saturdays and Sundays are assigned as working days in order to cluster more days off around holidays. Longer holiday blocks (e.g., Chinese New Year holiday, week of National Day) are often referred to as a “golden week” for their potential in tourism and commerce.
