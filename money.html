Money & Banking

Getting money into China takes planning – wires, ATMs, and WeChat all have quirks that are easier to avoid than fix after the fact. Adding credit cards to use in WeChat is simple, but US banks often block charges from China. WeChat can often block charges from hitting the banks.

Moving Money via Wire Transfer

Funds can be wired in USD or RMB – the currency you choose changes the process significantly.

Wiring USD

  • Requires an in-person bank visit to exchange it into RMB
  • ~$50,000 annual deposit limit
  • Expect a fair amount of paperwork and signatures during the exchange

Wiring RMB

  • No exchange step required
  • No annual deposit limit
  • Wiring bank will ask for paperwork on the money's origin/purpose

Account Holder Name Format Matters

Recall exactly how your name is formatted on your bank paperwork – "LastFirst" or with a space, and where the capital letters fall. WeChat requires an exact match to link your card or bank account.

Other Methods to Move Money

Don't forget to notify your bank.

Call customer service proactively to whitelist international payments. If you have authorized users on the same card, simultaneous payments may also trigger a block.

Wise App (Recommended)

Transfer funds from your US bank to a Chinese bank (via UnionPay ATM card) using Wise. You can transfer up to RMB¥30,000 (about USD$4,400) per transfer with about a USD$100 transfer fee. Initial setup requires passport-image and face-scan verification, but subsequent transfers are quicker.

ATM Transfers

Usually you can only withdraw RMB¥3,500 per trip (about USD$500), and there may be a fee on top of that.

Transfers via WeChat from US Banks

WeChat's own transfer feature claims to accept direct transfers from a US account, but it hasn't worked well in practice – stick to Wise or ICBC for now.

Emergency Funding

In most places, it seems wise to have cash for emergencies, but here cash is rarely even seen. In fact, it may not even be a reliable backup as some vendors won't accept it. A more reliable backup is to ensure both WeChat and AliPay work and both have at least two credit/debit cards loaded. Further, you'll be better prepared if you also have access to one or more of these: a bank account, a balance loaded to WeChat, and a Relative Card issued in WeChat from someone with a bank account.
