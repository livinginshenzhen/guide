Apps You'll Need

WeChat isn't optional. It's your wallet, delivery service, transit pass, and social hub. Here's how to hit the ground running with that app – and all the others that will be important.

Payments & Cross-Border Money Transfers See the Money tab for information on wiring funds, WeChat card linking, and Wise/ICBC transfer options.

WeChat

Install this first. Everything in China runs on it, in it, and through it.

Be aware: WeChat is not end-to-end encrypted (Source: The Citizen Lab, University of Toronto). Messages should not be treated as private.

Useful WeChat Mini Programs

Think of mini programs as apps but apps inside of WeChat. To find them, swipe down from the top in WeChat. They are useful because mini programs can auto translate. You'll find the translate button under the secondary button (looks like three dots or sometimes a location symbol).

Essential Apps

As with mini programs, note that apps regularly change their icon artwork – which does everything for eyeballs and nothing for recognition.

Notes on App Store Regions

Apple App Store: Some apps (like Sam's Club (China)) require switching your Apple ID's region to China. Apple's instructions. Only switch back to the US region store once the app has fully downloaded – switching back too early can interrupt it.
Google Play: Inaccessible inside China without a VPN.
Samsung Galaxy Store: Auto-switches to the Chinese store when a Chinese SIM is detected, and is only in Chinese characters.
