Packing to Move

This is what and how much you can ship.

Air Cargo Shipping

Usually the allotment is 100 cubic feet (2.83 m³) per person – roughly 20 large moving boxes, though packaging material eats into that fast. The shipment usually doesn't leave the US until your residence permit is granted, so expect delay even after you've landed. It also isn't airtight, so sachets of activated charcoal in each box (especially ones with fabric) helps with the musty smell that sets in after weeks in a warehouse. We've seen it take 5 weeks from residency permit to delivery fairly consistently.

What Cannot Ship

  • Valuables – cash, jewelry of any value, precious stones, high-value collectibles, silverware
  • Pressure-sensitive goods – food, beverages or alcohol, toiletries, cosmetics, aerosols/perfumes, cartridges (including printers with cartridges installed)
  • Books – capped at 50 regardless of family size, due to weight/density
  • Specific items – cameras, drones, weapons of any kind, refrigerators containing Freon, medicine, toxic/flammable/radioactive materials, religious content/items, any newly-purchased packaged items
  • Most magnets – if it would deflect a compass placed next to it, it's too magnetic to ship
  • Any batteries – items can ship with batteries removed, but the batteries themselves have to travel with you in cabin luggage

Visualize the 100 cubic feet allotment against everyday items at upakweship.com before you start packing – it's easy to overestimate how much that actually is.
