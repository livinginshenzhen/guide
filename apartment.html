Your Address

Notice that addresses are written from broader to detailed – the opposite of the US. Most services just ask you to paste your whole address into their particular interface and it'll take it from there. You don't necessarily need to know the parts and usually won't be asked for the postal code.

Electrical Outlets & Plugs

The apartments have too few outlets. Here are the plug types you'll find:

Type A / Type I

One two-flat-pin outlet and one three-pin outlet. Any two-pin US plugs (most consumer electronics and chargers) will work in the Type A slot. Most of the outlets are this style.

Type A / I + USB-A

Same as previous with built-in USB-A ports for charging without an adapter.

Universal

Accepts Type A, Type C, and Type I, but there aren't many of these.

Voltage Mismatch

The US uses 110V / 60Hz and China uses 220V / 50Hz. The difference is significant enough to destroy non-compatible devices (e.g., small appliances), but most modern consumer electronics will be dual-voltage. If you bring a US-only appliance and plug it into an outlet, it will be damaged or destroyed.

Safe to Plug In (Dual-Voltage)

Most modern electronics auto-detect and handle both voltages. Look for "100-240V" on the label:

  • Laptops
  • Phone & tablet chargers
  • Camera battery chargers
  • Most modern power adapters

Do NOT Plug In (Single-Voltage)

These items underperform or be a fire hazard. Check the label.

  • Cheap hair dryers
  • Some hair straighteners and curling irons
  • Older kitchen appliances
  • Anything with a fixed-speed motor
