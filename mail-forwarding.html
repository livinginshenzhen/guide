Mail Forwarding

Mail from your last home address – but not packages – can be shipped to you in country.

How It Works

1

You need to complete a Change of Address form at a nearby post office or online (includes a nominal authentication fee).

2

The company Mail & Print Solutions department collects the mail from USPS and sorts envelopes, letters, and magazines from anything deemed junk (e.g., mailers, current resident addressees).

3

Once a week that mail is shipped to the in-country office location.

Address for Overseas Assignment Mail Forwarding:

P.O. Box 1707

(City, state, and postal code are the same as the office.)

Questions to Consider

  1. Who is moving? Filing as “Family” will forward mail to any recipient with the same surname.
  2. Will you return in six months? “No” classifies as a six-month maximum “permanent” change of address.

Tips

  1. A six-month duration will be automatic, but can in some situations be 12 months.
  2. Keep track of the expiration date, and plan to renew online before that period is over.
  3. The post office will allow 30 months with a fee.
  4. A key contact person is Stephanie Chinn (email address available in the directory).
  5. Any new Change of Address submission overrides any existing forwarding, so complete the form using your home address as ‘from’ and the company PO Box as the ‘to’ as if any current arrangement does not exist.
