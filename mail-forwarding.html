Mail Forwarding

Mail from your last home address – but not packages – can be shipped to you in country.

How It Works

1

When mail is sent to your last home address, it is forwarded to a company USPS post office box.

2

When it arrives, the company’s Mail & Print Solutions department sorts envelopes, letters, and magazines from anything deemed junk (e.g., flyers, mail to ‘current occupant’).

3

Once a week that mail is shipped as one batch to your in-country office location to sort locally.

Setting Up Mail Forwarding

  • You need to complete a Change of Address form at a nearby post office or online (includes a nominal authentication fee).
  • You’ll be asked, “Who is moving?” Filing as “Family” will forward mail to any recipient with the same surname.
  • You’ll be asked, “Will you return in six months?” (See below diagrams.) Answering “No” categorizes it as “Permanent,” which forwards mail for 12 months and can be extended up to 30 months.
  • Although you should be mailed a postcard reminder, keep track of the expiration date on your own and plan to renew online before that period is over.
  • Note that during the mail forwarding period categorized as “Permanent,” any senders that include “Address Service Requested” on their envelope will be notified of your address change. You may need to change some addresses individually, for example, with any account or service that changed your address in their system based on that notification.

Withdrawing Mail Forwarding

  • To restore mail delivery to your home after a “Permanent” change of address, you need to fill out another Change of Address form in reverse – forward it from the P.O. Box to your home.
  • After 12 months (or longer, if you’ve extended it), mail will be returned to sender with a “forward time expired” stamp for 6 subsequent months.

Notes about Changes of Address

  • A “Permanent Change of Address” does not mean it’s irreversible. It simply means there is not a return date determined in the short term. At any point you can file a Change of Address to essentially discontinue mail forwarding.
  • Any new Change of Address submission overrides any existing forwarding.
