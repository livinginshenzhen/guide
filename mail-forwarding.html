Mail Forwarding

Mail from your last home address – but not packages – can be shipped to you in country.

How It Works

1

When mail is sent to your last home address, it is forwarded to a company USPS post office box.

2

When it arrives, the company’s Mail & Print Solutions department sorts envelopes, letters, and magazines from anything deemed junk (e.g., flyers, mail to ‘current occupant’).

3

Once a week that mail is shipped as one batch to your in-country office location to sort locally.

Setting Up Mail Forwarding

  • You need to complete a Change of Address form at a nearby post office or online (includes a nominal authentication fee).
  • You’ll be asked, “Who is moving?” Filing as “Family” will forward mail to any recipient with the same surname.
  • You’ll be asked, “Will you return in six months?” Answering “Yes” categorizes it as “Temporary,” which forwards mail for 6 months at a time at which point a new change of address can be filed.
  • Although you should be mailed a postcard reminder, keep track of the expiration date on your own and plan to renew online before that period is over.

Withdrawing Mail Forwarding

Any new Change of Address submission overrides any existing forwarding.
