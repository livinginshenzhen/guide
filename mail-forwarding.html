Mail Forwarding

Mail from your last home address – but not packages – can be shipped to you in country.

How It Works

1

You need to complete a Change of Address form at a nearby post office or online (includes a nominal authentication fee).

2

The company Mail & Print Solutions department collects the mail from USPS and sorts envelopes, letters, and magazines from anything deemed junk (e.g., flyers, mail to ‘current occupant’).

3

Once a week that mail is shipped as one batch to the in-country office location to sort locally.

Address for Overseas Assignment Mail Forwarding:

P.O. Box 1707

(City, state, and postal code are the same as the office.)

Questions to Consider

  1. Who is moving? Filing as “Family” will forward mail to any recipient with the same surname.
  2. Will you return in six months? Answering “No” categorizes it as “Permanent,” which forwards mail for at least 12 months and can be extended up to 30 months.

Tips for Mail Forwarding

  1. A “Permanent Change of Address” does not mean irreversible. It simply means there is not a return date determined in the short term. At any point you can file a Change of Address to essentially discontinue mail forwarding.
  2. Keep track of the expiration date, and plan to renew online before that period is over.
  3. A key contact person is Stephanie Chinn (email address available in the directory).
  4. Any new Change of Address submission overrides any existing forwarding, so complete the form using your home address as ‘from’ and the company P.O. Box as the ‘to’ as if any current arrangement does not exist.
