Internet Access

China actively defends against foreign internet traffic. All traffic leaving or entering China is inspected – foreign services are blocked, VPNs are unreliable, and speeds to foreign servers are slow. Local apps like WeChat operate on infrastructure that bypasses this entirely.

Questions To Answer

A whole-house VPN router makes sense if you're home often and need consistent US access. If you mostly need occasional secure browsing, a VPN app on your phone may be enough – with the caveats below.

Recommendation

Get your own VPN service before you arrive. Once inside China, it can be very difficult to download and set up a VPN. An international eSIM is your most reliable fallback when VPNs fail.

What's Blocked in China (Without a VPN)

The Great Firewall blocks these services entirely for everyone inside China. A working VPN is required to access them.

Google

Search, Gmail, Maps, Drive, YouTube, Meet

Meta / Facebook

Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Threads

Yahoo

Yahoo Mail, Yahoo News, Yahoo Finance

Microsoft (partial)

OneDrive & some Bing services may be unreliable. Outlook & Teams generally work.

Your VPN Options

Commercial VPN

Work some of the time. The GFW actively detects and blocks VPN server IPs. Enforcement intensifies around politically sensitive periods.

Currently working (as of 2026):

ExpressVPN Astrill Surfshark*

*Surfshark works occasionally

Private VPN (Most Reliable)

Coordinate a private VPN connection leveraging a service or hosting your own solution, which will be a project. While this solution may be the most reliable, it can be expensive or may require a lot of work to get working. Ask for advice from the community.

International eSIM (Best Fallback)

Routes through non-Chinese infrastructure, bypassing the GFW entirely. Not fast or cheap enough to be a primary connection, but works even when ISPs cut off foreign connections.

See the Mobile tab for eSIM setup details.

Internet Access in Apartment

Captive Hotel WiFi Network

Included. Works for basic browsing but the built-in VPN is not refreshed often – use CM+ to bootstrap your own personal VPN, not as your primary solution.

1 Gbps + bonus SIM card. VPN not included. You will need to provide your own wifi router.

You may need a Mesh WiFi Router.

The apartment walls are thick. A single router often isn't enough coverage – plan for a mesh network if you set up your own connection.

VPN Availability

Access is entirely unpredictable. For example, when a sensitive event is taking place, expect VPN capabilities to be spotty. After April 2026, WireGuard was actively blocked.

Blocked Even On Many VPNs

You simply can't count on any one VPN option, but note that these services are known to block access based on location.
